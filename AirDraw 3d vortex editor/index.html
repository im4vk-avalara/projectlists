🎯 HOW TO DRAW

1 Point your index finger ☝️ (curl other fingers)
2 Cursor turns RED = Drawing mode active
3 Move your finger to draw the shape
4 Open your palm 🖐️ to STOP drawing
5 Shape auto-recognized: Line, Rectangle, Circle, Arc
LINE: Draw straight
RECT: Draw closed box
CIRCLE: Draw closed curve

Quick Tips

  • 🔴 Red cursor = Drawing mode (pen down)
  • 🟢 Green cursor = Hover mode (pen up)
  • Draw slowly and steadily for best recognition
  • Close your shape (end near start) for rectangles/circles
